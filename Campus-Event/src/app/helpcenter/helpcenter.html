Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for common administrative tasks and issues

I forgot my password.How do I reset it?

Go to Login Page → Click "Forgot Password?" → Enter your registered email → Submit → Check your email for the password reset link → Follow the link to set a new password.

Which role should I choose?

Go to Account Setup → Role Selection Section → Review role descriptions carefully → Select the role that matches your responsibilities (e.g., Admin, Coordinator, User, Host) → Save changes.

How do I contact support?

Go to Help Center → Click "Contact Support" → Fill in the form with your query → Submit → Wait for the support team to respond via email.

How do I change my account settings?

Go to Profile → Click "Account Settings" → Edit the fields you want to update (e.g., Email, Password, Preferences) → Save changes.

Can I switch between different roles?

Go to Profile → Role Management Section → Select the desired role from the dropdown → Confirm role change → Log out and log back in to activate the new role.

Why cant I access certain features?

Go to Role & Permission Settings → Check assigned role and its permissions → If needed, contact Admin → Request role or permission upgrade → Admin updates your role or grants access → Re-login to see the features.

About CampusConnect

The Campus Event Management Application is designed to simplify the way events are organized and experienced within the campus. It brings students, faculty, and organizers together on a single platform where upcoming events can be discovered, registered for, and managed with ease. The application ensures smooth scheduling, better participation, and hassle-free coordination, making campus life more engaging and connected.
